MoxaSerial @VERSION@ — DNC over RS‑232 through a Moxa NPort, for Autodesk Fusion.

This installer copies the add-in into your personal Fusion add-ins folder:

~/Library/Application Support/Autodesk/Autodesk Fusion 360/API/AddIns/MoxaSerial

Nothing is installed system-wide and no administrator password is needed. An add-in already in that folder is renamed to MoxaSerial.bak‑<date> before the new one is written.

Quit Fusion before continuing if it is running — otherwise you will need to restart it, or stop and re-run the add-in, before the new version is loaded.

Your machine settings and logs live separately in ~/Library/Application Support/MoxaSerial/ and are left untouched by this installer.
